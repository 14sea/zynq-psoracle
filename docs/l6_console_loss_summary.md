# (historical) design-review section, 2026-09-01 — the three C1 sessions, denominators corrected in the review

Generated by `host/l6_loss_stats.py` from the three C1 evidence directories; numbers in
`evidence/l6_console_loss_stats.json`. **Three loss events across C1 #1 and C1 #3; C1 #2
read nothing and contributes no bytes.** With three events, a position is not a pattern:
two of three landing on chunk 3 is NOT read as a chunk-3 fault.

Loss rate: **3 events over 630 full-size chunk transmissions = 0.48 %**

## The events

| session | seq | chunk | shape | bytes missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 #1 | 24 | 4 → 5 | a loss spanning the line boundary: chunk 4's tail and chunk 5's head merged into one 6002-byte line (7 fields → `FrameError` → collector `CRASHED`) | ≈ 39 |
| C1 #3 | 20 | 3 | a contiguous run missing inside the payload; the line kept its shape, failed CRC, was dropped | 309 |
| C1 #3 | 62 | 3 | same shape | 229 |

Common to all three: each fell inside a full-size ~3 KB audit line, during the audit burst
(8 lines back-to-back, ~24 KB, ~1.85 s at 115 200 baud); each is a contiguous deletion,
not a bit flip; CRC caught the two that stayed inside a line, and the line-boundary one was
caught as a malformed frame. The path is CH340 → usbipd → WSL `vhci_hcd`, no flow control.

## What the zero fraction means for the design

96.4 % of the audited words are zero. A lossless sparse encoding of the
same 2814 words (`docs/l6_audit_pull_design.md`: unlisted positions are zero, listed
positions strictly ascending and unique) keeps the audit whole — the host still rebuilds
all 2814 words and recomputes the three hashes — while cutting the bytes on the wire by
**about 80 %** (framing, token and CRC per line remain: on C1 #3's 64 complete audits the
sparse wire is 4 282–4 484 bytes against 22 320, i.e. 19–20 %), and with them about 80 %
of the link time in which the loss events occurred.
