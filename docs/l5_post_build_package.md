## 4. The audit condition, restated

`all-self-reporting`. Every candidate that **staged** is audited before its own record is
emitted — including a **link-2 refusal**, whose entire claim is `staged != commit` and which
the host could not otherwise check; its audit carries the staging streams and says so
(`span: "streams"`). A candidate refused by the **gate** staged nothing, so no raw words
exist: it is **exempt and recorded as exempt**, corroborated instead by the notary log's own
refusal under rule (vii). `verified: "audited"` means the words were **served**.

The earlier wording — "the first session audits every candidate" — was not implementable and
has been replaced rather than quietly under-delivered.

## 5. Limitations, stated as limitations

1. **The firmware has never executed.** Nothing here is evidence about the PL, the transport
   or timing.
2. **`p3_app.c`'s *use* of the wire unit is checked statically**, not dynamically: that file
   is MMIO- and BSP-bound and does not compile on the host. The guards are in
   `tests/test_firmware_audit.py` class `WireWiring`, named static for that reason.
3. **The audit request arrives in advance**, so at rates below 100 % this is weaker than a
   surprise post-hoc audit. At this session's rate every self-reporting candidate is audited.
   A surprise audit of an *earlier* candidate needs the W-deep ring plus a way to record the
   result without the collector rewriting the application's self-report; not built, not
   claimed.
4. **`fclk0_hz_decoded` is host-supplied and echoed**, not an application measurement: the
   source audit permits exactly one SLCR symbol (the IDCODE read) and that guard was not
   weakened to add a field.
5. **`CPU_6x4x` / `PERIPHCLK` remain assumptions** (6:2:1) until the blocking preflight
   `md.l 0xF8000120 1` at first power-on. Nothing in a watchdog-off session depends on them,
   and the runner performs and stores that read before anything else.
6. **The evidence ring is not implemented.** `P3_RING_ADDR`/`P3_RING_W` are declared and
   TLB-mapped; audits are served from the live buffers of the candidate in flight.

## 6. If this package passes

In one batch, in this order: push the local commits (currently 12 ahead of
`origin/main` `90566b4`, `90566b4` itself still awaiting ratification) → owner creates
`whole-of-probe P3-L5` and `provisioning P3-K` → power cycle → re-run
`host/verify_principal_boundary.py` as the runner (< 6 h) → **preflight `CPU_CLK_CTRL`** →
run `host/l5_runner.py --audit-all --budget 8` in the background, no shell timeout, waited on
by pid.

A `CRASHED` end is sealed, never repaired; PASS/HOLD/KILL are fixed in `docs/l5_prereg.md`
and are not to be renegotiated against the result.
